A letter for my sister

Not everything important is easy to say out loud. So I made you a little space for it here.

To my favourite sister · with love

Dear Neha,

Happy birthday, Neha. ♡ I hope you know how special you are—not just today, but on all the regular days too. You deserve the kind of happiness that stays after the cake is gone, the photos are taken, and the celebrations get quiet.

I may not say it every day, but I’m always wishing the best for you. I hope this new year of your life brings you peace in your heart, confidence in your dreams, good people around you, and so many reasons to laugh that your cheeks hurt.

Keep being your own kind of wonderful. Keep growing, exploring, smiling, and becoming everything you want to be. No matter how much life changes, you’ll always have a brother cheering for you, rooting for you, and wanting to see you happy.

And one more thing: you never have to earn your place in my heart. It’s already yours. I hope this tiny birthday corner reminds you that you are loved more than a webpage could ever fit into words.

Always in your corner,
Shivam ♡

P.S. Save a piece of cake for me. ♡
